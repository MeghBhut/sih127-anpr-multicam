The pipeline

A frame enters at the far left and leaves as a row in the sightings table. Nothing is identified in one pass: the top band runs many times per vehicle, collecting noisy plate reads, and only hands the bottom band a result once voting locks it or the OCR budget runs out.

Many bad reads, one plate

The linker rejects before it scores

Linking two sightings is the only place the system can put an innocent car's number on someone else's route. So four hard filters run first, each returning a flat zero and a reason. A pair reaches the weighted score only after surviving all four.

Who owns what

Signatures are frozen. Every threshold lives in config.py, so tuning never means editing someone else's module.

What this prototype does not do

Stated openly rather than hidden. These belong on the feasibility slide.

  • Detection

    Auto-rickshaws are not a COCO class. They currently come out as car or motorcycle, which then breaks the same-type filter in the linker.

  • OCR

    Two-line bike plates, painted truck plates and night glare all read poorly with the pretrained model. Failing crops are saved for a future fine-tuning set.

  • Thresholds

    Every weight and cutoff is a placeholder until roughly 50 hand-labelled pairs exist. The target is precision at or above 0.98, chosen over recall on purpose.

  • Routes

    The path between two cameras is inferred, never observed. The map draws it dashed for exactly that reason.

  • Identity

    An embedding-only link is low confidence and is never used alone to identify a vehicle. Without a plate and without an embedding, no link is written at all.
